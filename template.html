    Main-round figures exclude sibling applicants. Charter schools run their own lotteries and publish no counts, so they show a rating only.

    How the odds are built. Combined odds = main-round success + (1 − main-round success) × waitlist odds. Main-round success means being assigned this program or one the family ranked higher. Sibling applicants are taken out of every main-round figure, because they are placed almost every time: each rate is (all applicants − sibling applicants) on both the top and the bottom. The neighborhood figure is the sheet's attendance-area rate as published, which cannot be split by sibling status. Waitlist odds are offers made so far divided by the current waitlist length, capped at 100%, and the same waitlist odds are applied to every applicant whatever their tiebreakers.

    City-wide or assigned neighborhood. SFUSD treats K-8 schools, SF Public Montessori, schools where every student is in a language or newcomer program, early education schools, middle schools and high schools as city-wide: they have no attendance area, so living nearby gives no tiebreaker. Language programs inside a neighborhood school are city-wide programs too. General education TK feeder programs at early education schools are the exception: they give a tiebreaker to families in the feeder elementary school's attendance area. The split here follows the sheet, marking a program as neighborhood-based when it has attendance-area applicants.

    Special education (SDC) programs and Access SFUSD sites are left out. Counts in the district sheet are rounded, so its percentages can differ slightly from the counts shown.

    Sources: SFUSD main round results, waitlist lengths (updated __UPDATED__) and city-wide schools. Ratings and school locations from GreatSchools, read November 19, 2026. Neighborhood outlines from DataSF.

    Charter schools. The 12 charter schools on the map are public and open to any San Francisco family, but each runs its own application. Their admissions details were gathered from each school's website and other public pages in November 2026, so confirm dates with the school. Five Keys, which serves adults and people in custody, is left out, as is Mary L. Booker Leadership Academy, which the state lists as closed.
